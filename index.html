How to play

  1. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard.
  2. Move a token the number of steps shown on the dice.
  3. The glowing dice belongs to whoever's turn it is — tap it to roll.
  4. If you only have one legal move, it plays automatically.
  5. Land on an opponent (not on a ★ or start square) to send it home.
  6. Rolling a 6 gives you another roll — but three 6's in a row forfeits your turn.
  7. Get all 4 tokens to the center to win!
